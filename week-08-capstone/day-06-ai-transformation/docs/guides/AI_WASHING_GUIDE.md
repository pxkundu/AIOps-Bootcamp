# AI Washing Guide: Defending Against "AI Theater"
How to spot and filter out low-value AI hype in your vendor portfolio.

---

## 🎭 What is AI Washing?

As noted in the **Glean AI Transformation 100** report, many vendors "cloak shaky products in buzzwords that sound smart but mean little." This is **AI Washing** — and it creates "AI Theater" where organizations pay for labels instead of results.

### The 5-Part AI Washing Gut Check:
Use the **Innovation Scanner Agent** to put every new AI proposal through these 5 tests.

| # | Test | Question | Passing Signal |
|---|------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | **Outlandish Promises Test** | Can they show working AI today, or is it all "on the roadmap"? | Working demo with real-world data. |
| 2 | **AI Residue Test** | If you remove every instance of the word "AI" from the pitch, do you still have an impressive story? | The product provides value even without the AI tag. |
| 3 | **Reference Ghosting Test** | Can they give you the name of at least 3 current, happy, paying customers? | Verified enterprise references (not "strategic partners"). |
| 4 | **Human-in-the-Loop Test** | Does the product assume all humans disappear, or does it enhance human judgment? | Clear role for human review and feedback. |
| 5 | **Missing Metric Test** | Is every positive claim tied to a specific, measurable outcome? | Quantified targets (e.g., "reduce latency by 30%"). |

---

## 🚫 Red Flags to Watch For:
- **Jargon Monoxide**: Overuse of "transformative," "smarter," "agentic," and "generative" without explaining *how*.
- **The "Strategic Partner" Dodge**: When asked for references, they send you to a friendly collaborator instead of a real user.
- **The Roadmap Trap**: Every key feature requested is "coming in Q3."

---

## 📈 Innovation Portfolio Health

A healthy enterprise AI portfolio should follow the **VC Model**:
- **Expect Failure**: 70-80% of your AI experiments *should* fail. If every project is a "success," you're not taking enough risks — or you're grading too easy.
- **Probabilistic Betting**: Allocate budget based on statistical probability of success, not just the best "pitch."
- **Measure actual ROI**: Verified hours/costs saved, not just "projected" numbers.
