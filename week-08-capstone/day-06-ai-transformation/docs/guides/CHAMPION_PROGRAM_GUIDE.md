# AI Champion Program Guide: Building a Peer-to-Peer Learning Network
How to surface and empower the right people in your organization to drive AI adoption.

---

## 🏆 What is an AI Champion?

Based on the **Babson College research by Rob Cross**, top-down change typically reaches just 30-35% of an organization. Pairing formal efforts with internal "AI Influencers" can be **twice as effective**.

An **AI Champion** is someone who is:
- **Curious**: Always testing new models, prompts, and workflows.
- **Credible**: Respected by their immediate peers for their actual work output.
- **Visible**: Well-connected within their team or department.

---

## 🔍 Identifying Your Champions (Behavior vs. Title)

Do not ask managers to nominate "champions" — they may pick favorites. Instead, follow the **Glean AI Transformation 100** playbook and **watch behavior**.

### 1. Run a Prompt-a-thon or Agent-a-thon
Launch a 48-hour challenge. Watch:
- Who dives in first?
- Who helps others with their prompts?
- Who collaborates across departments?
- **Most Important**: Who implements the best ideas AFTER the challenge ends?

### 2. The Champion Score Formula
Use the **Champion Finder Agent** to analyze:
- **Usage Frequency**: (Weight 30%) Who uses AI tools 5+ times per week?
- **Sharing Ratio**: (Weight 30%) Who shares AI tips/prompts in Slack/Teams?
- **Implementation Rate**: (Weight 40%) Who actually ships AI-driven code or workflows?

---

## 🚀 Empowering the Network

Once you've identified your champions (aim for 1-2 per team), equip them for success.

### 1. The "Train the Trainer" Model
- **Early Access**: Give champions early access to new models (GPT-5, Gemini 2.0, Claude 4.0).
- **Deep Training**: Provide advanced prompt engineering and agent building workshops.
- **Direct Feedback**: Connect champions directly with your AI implementation team.

### 2. Lateral Adoption Rituals
- **AI Showcase**: 5 minutes at every staff meeting for a champion to demo "What I did with AI this week."
- **Internal AI Wiki**: Champions contribute to a shared library of successful enterprise prompts.
- **Champion Office Hours**: Weekly sessions where peers can ask "How do I automate this?"

---

## ✅ Deliverables of the Program
- [ ] A map of internal AI influencers across all business units.
- [ ] A curated repository of shared enterprise-specific prompts.
- [ ] Documented cases where champions have solved specific team friction.
- [ ] Measured jump in team-wide AI usage (Target: 75% adoption where manager visibility is high).
