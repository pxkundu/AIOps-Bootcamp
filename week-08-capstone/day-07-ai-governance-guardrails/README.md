# Week 8 Day 7: AI Implementation, Guardrails & Governance

> **Duration:** 8 hours | **Difficulty:** Advanced  
> **Theme:** Production-grade AI requires **implementation discipline**, **technical guardrails**, and **organizational governance**—not model choice alone.

---

## What you will do

1. Study how to **implement** AI systems safely (lifecycle, data, evaluation, operations).
2. Design **guardrails** (input/output policy, tool limits, safety filters, human approval).
3. Put **governance** in place (roles, policies, audit, risk tiers, compliance hooks).
4. Build the **capstone codebase**: a minimal **AI Governance Control Plane** that enforces policies and logs decisions.
