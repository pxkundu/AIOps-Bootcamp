## 🧠 Theory: Conversational Infrastructure
Why do we "chat" with servers?
1. **Context Sharing**: No more "I ran a command on my machine and it worked." The history is public.
2. **Standardization**: Buttons enforce standard procedures. You can't "misspell" a button.
3. **Speed**: One click is faster than three logic gates and five SSH commands.
